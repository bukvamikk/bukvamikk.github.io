Hi, I'm Miklós Bukva

UX/UI designer.

I started as a developer and spent years in frontend development, mainly with React and Bootstrap. I moved into design because I wanted to shape what gets built, not just how.

In recent years I've been working as a UX/UI designer at an international software company in Vienna. My focus areas are design systems, research-led design and accessibility.

I'm interested in how AI can speed up research and design. My rule is simple: do no harm. Humans stay in control, it's always clear what the AI does, and I only use it where it makes the product better.

What I care about

  • Clarity. If something needs explaining, I redesign it.
  • For everyone. A good website is easy for anyone to use, whatever their age, eyesight or device.
  • Measurable results. Good design is worth it when it brings more enquiries, sales or happy users.
