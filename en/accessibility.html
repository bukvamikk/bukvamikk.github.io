Accessibility statement

I want everyone to be able to use this site easily, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Conformance level

This website is built to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What I've done

  • Semantic HTML, a clear heading hierarchy and landmarks.
  • Full keyboard support, a clearly visible focus indicator and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1, and above 7:1 in most places, in both light and dark mode.
  • A highly legible typeface (Atkinson Hyperlegible), designed for readers with low vision.
  • Text can be zoomed to 200%, and the site works at 320 pixels wide without horizontal scrolling.
  • Touch targets of at least 44×44 pixels.
  • Animations switch off when reduced motion is set in your system.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and error messages are shown as text and announced by screen readers.
  • No tracking cookies; if you switch to dark mode, only your browser remembers that choice.

How it was tested

With automated checks (axe-core), keyboard walkthroughs and contrast measurement. I retest the site regularly.

Known limitations

I'm not currently aware of any issue that prevents use of the site.

Feedback

If you run into any barrier, write to hello@bukva.design. I reply within 5 working days and fix the problem as soon as I can.
