Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

A one-page landing site takes 1 week at most, a small business site 3 weeks at most, and a company site or webshop 3–6 weeks. We fix the exact schedule in the proposal.

Do I have to make my website accessible?

If you run a webshop or provide certain online services, then under the European Accessibility Act you most likely have had to since 28 June 2025. Exceptions may apply to microenterprises. A quick audit shows where your site stands; for legal questions, it's worth checking with a lawyer too.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. We pick a content management system that suits you, and at launch I show you how to change text and images.

What do I need for the consultation?

Nothing special. If you have one, bring your current site's address, a few websites you like, and what you want to achieve. We'll work out the rest together.

How does payment work?

Usually 50% upfront and 50% at launch. Larger projects are paid by milestone.

Do you work with clients outside Budapest or abroad?

Yes. We can meet online or in person, so I'm happy to work with clients anywhere in Hungary or abroad, in Hungarian or English.

Not sure which package you need?

We'll figure it out together in a free consultation. Then I'll send you a fixed-price proposal.
